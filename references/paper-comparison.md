# Post-Freeze Paper and Expert Comparison

This reference is opt-in. Read and apply it only after the user explicitly requests retrospective training or comparison for a past problem. Do not infer authorization from the problem's year, title, familiarity, or the existence of award papers, and do not offer this comparison as the default continuation of an independent strategy task.

## Evidence order

Use sources in this order:

1. official problem and attachments;
2. problem author or official problem commentary;
3. official judging/assessment report;
4. award-verified high-level papers;
5. official follow-up research or later domain work.

Later sources may improve a method but cannot rewrite earlier statement facts. Do not label an official showcase paper as a national-first paper unless the award is independently verified.

## Minimum comparison discipline

When the user requests a multi-paper comparison, seek at least three genuinely relevant sources when available. Diversity matters more than count: prefer different modeling routes, not three copies of the same idea.

For every source record:

- identity and verification status;
- what it understood correctly;
- what it omitted, assumed, or possibly misread;
- route strengths and failure risks;
- evidence that can improve the independent route;
- content that should not be copied.

## Separate two judgments

### Problem understanding

Compare object definition, information boundary, subquestion dependencies, hard constraints, objective semantics, output fields, ambiguity handling, and fatal counterexamples.

### Solution method

Compare mathematical closure, identifiability, baseline quality, algorithm appropriateness, constraint feasibility, data capacity, validation, robustness, computational burden, and paper readability.

Never allow a sophisticated method to compensate for a wrong interpretation.

## Generalization rule

Promote a lesson only when it can be stated without the historical problem's name, constants, or answer; has a clear trigger; survives another structurally different case; and does not make prior cases worse. Otherwise retain it only as case evidence.

## Copyright and evidence integrity

Store summaries, logic maps, page references, and hashes rather than copying entire protected papers into the skill. Open the local paper card, gallery, or original only when exact evidence is needed. Do not quote long passages.
