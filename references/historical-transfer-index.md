## Cross-case preservation rules learned from all 34

1. Do not stop by model count; stop by zero marginal responsibility or value.
2. Statistical sample limits learned freedom, not statement-forced mechanics.
3. Solver complexity is separate from model complexity; nonconvex/event/discrete tasks may need global generation, local refinement, and independent replay.
4. Similar outputs can still carry different proof duties: candidate, lower bound, error bound, or independent verification.
5. Different subquestions and information regimes may legitimately require different model families.
6. Historical papers supply counterexamples and implementation ideas; they do not override the current statement.
